# Plan 12 — Cross-Era Evaluation and Recurrence Synthesis

Status: queued. Depends on completion of Plans 08–11, including documented insufficient-evidence closeouts.

## Objective

Integrate formal prior-iteration reviews with the bounded 2022–2027 evaluation. Answer which objectives improved, persisted, evolved or were replaced across actual governing versions.

## Work

1. Build an era/objective crosswalk distinguishing true continuity from similar wording, new scope, changed targets or changed outcome definitions.
2. Assemble the master matrix with separate rows for each confirmed/reconstructed version and objective. Keep the existing current-plan findings visible and label incomplete historical authority.
3. Classify recurrence using matched period evidence: sustained responsibility, evolving challenge, persistent unresolved problem, recurrent implementation failure, measurement failure, resolved/institutionalized. Require explicit execution/fidelity evidence for recurrent implementation failure; repeat task labels alone are insufficient.
4. Distinguish improvement in outcome levels from narrowed subgroup gaps and improvement relative to Washington. Maintain incompatible/suppressed/missing contexts, not synthetic averages.
5. Rewrite the final report around the resulting across-era scope, with evidence quality, contrary evidence, adoption/measurement gaps and actionable next evidence. Do not turn domain task completion into whole-plan attainment or produce a composite score without a separately justified method.

## Outputs / acceptance

Cross-era narrative and machine-readable matrix, assertion/evidence index, updated README/history scope and finite reopening conditions. Prior matrices trace to sources/calculations; no new collection in synthesis unless a specific contradiction requires correction. Existing 2022–2027 synthesis remains clearly identified as that review, not silently overwritten as evidence of all prior iterations.

## Stop

All known candidate iterations have an evaluation or documented insufficient-evidence result. Remaining gaps are explicit. Research complete only for that declared coverage; report exactly which eras/objectives are rated and which are not.
