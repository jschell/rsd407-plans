# Plan 10 — Reconstruct and Evaluate the 2014–2019 Era

Status: queued. Depends on Plan 08. Authority/adoption gap is unresolved; do not assume a single 2014–2019 plan exists.

## Objective

Determine which plan(s) governed this interval and evaluate supported objectives without fabricating a missing iteration.

## Work

1. Use inventory leads for refreshed/adopted plans around 2013–2015. Conduct a bounded search of Board adoption/revision records, district annual reports/status/business plans, archived plan pages and predecessor/successor references.
2. Record authority and exact coverage. A later retrospective description cannot silently substitute for the original plan; distinguish corroborated reconstruction from confirmed adoption.
3. If sufficient plan evidence exists, transcribe its objective/target/task register and evaluate matched implementation/fidelity/outcomes using that era's own deadlines. Use existing FY2017–2019 audit/finance evidence only for the objectives it actually tests.
4. Treat assessment transitions, cohort definitions and missing records explicitly. Separate policy targets from strategic objectives; do not back-project the revised 2025 reserve rule.
5. If governing plan authority remains insufficient, deliver a documented gap assessment and rate only verifiable objective-specific assertions. Do not create an overall plan rating from unrelated annual outcomes.

## Outputs / acceptance

Authority/gap narrative, objective matrix where supportable, assertion IDs, source/hash/archive provenance and any deterministic calculations. Include search terms/surfaces/date/boundary so non-discovery is testable and not claimed exhaustive. PDF review policy from Plan 08 applies.

## Stop

Close after targeted authority search plus one objective-specific evidence pass where feasible. Insufficient authority is a valid research result. No indefinite archive crawl, guessed plan, interpolated outcomes or employee/student profiles.
