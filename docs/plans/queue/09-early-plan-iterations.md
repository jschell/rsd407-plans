# Plan 09 — Evaluate Early Plan Iterations, 2009–2014

Status: queued. Depends on Plan 08. Candidate years are discovery boundaries; inventory establishes actual versions.

## Objective

Evaluate the adopted 2009–2010 plan and documented revisions/status iterations through 2013–2014. Treat 2008–2009 material as precursor context unless authority establishes a governing plan.

## Work

1. Transcribe each version's own goals, objectives, tasks, owner, target, baseline and deadline with source/page. Maintain revision links and preserve changes in wording/targets.
2. Produce an objective-by-objective evidence matrix for each confirmed/reconstructed iteration. Separate diagnosis, task suitability, implementation, fidelity, outcomes and recurrence. An annual status update is evidence for its governing plan, not automatically a separately adopted plan.
3. Reuse finance/audit anchors and any existing educational outcomes only when period, measure and population match. Acquire narrowly required historical assessment/growth/graduation, finance, staffing or operations evidence tied to specific objectives. Historical tests may be incompatible with SBAC/WCAS; no unsupported conversion to a common score.
4. Compare baseline to plan endpoint and contemporaneous Washington where appropriate. Record missing/suppressed rows without zero substitution. Do not use 2024–2025 results to judge a 2010 deadline.
5. Identify successor carry-forward: routine continuing duty versus unresolved outcome versus unverified implementation. Repeated wording alone does not establish failure.

## Outputs / acceptance

Era evaluation document(s), machine-readable per-iteration objective matrix, source manifests and reproducible derived results. Every objective rated under the existing framework or explicitly Cannot determine/Insufficient evidence. Ratings resolve through stable assertion IDs to source records; dates and comparability limits explicit. PDF review, URL/hash/archive policy from Plan 08 applies.

## Stop

One bounded primary-plan/status/outcome pass per confirmed iteration. Close missing implementation or incompatible outcomes as limitations. No blanket decade-long collection or individual student/staff data. Reopen only for specific material authoritative evidence.
