# Plan 11 — Evaluate the 2020–2025 Plan and Transition

Status: complete (bounded review; 2026-10-01). Depends on Plan 08.

## Objective

Formally evaluate the confirmed 2020–2025 plan, its separate business-plan implementation structure and transition to 2022–2027. Establish whether tasks were retained, revised, replaced or assessed before assigning attainment.

## Work

1. Recover authoritative plan/adoption and business/status versions; map goals to tasks and dates. Establish replacement/supersession from documents, not overlapping titles alone.
2. Create the objective matrix under the same evaluation framework, using each original objective's own targets. Preserve outcome goals versus activity outputs.
3. Reuse validated OSPI and finance sources where periods/definitions fit; identify only specific additional observations needed for missing objectives. Pandemic interruptions, assessment availability, graduation cohorts and changing denominators require explicit qualifications.
4. Evaluate implementation and fidelity with dated period-specific records. Later 2025/2026 reports may corroborate continuation but do not retrospectively prove earlier deadline attainment.
5. Compare focal-group outcomes and Washington context where matched; do not combine incompatible measures or attribute effects from temporal association. Reconcile transition outcomes with current-plan findings without double-counting shared tasks or sources.

## Outputs / acceptance

2020–2025 evaluation, source/page-linked objective matrix, transition crosswalk to 2022–2027, reproducible calculations and source preservation manifest. Every material conclusion has an assertion ID; unavailable targets/results yield explicit Cannot determine rather than an invented threshold. PDF review/URL/hash/archive policy from Plan 08 applies.

## Stop

One bounded plan/business/status/period-outcome pass. No new generalized OSPI pipeline unless a named material assertion cannot otherwise be tested. No waiting for future results or individual case reconstruction.
