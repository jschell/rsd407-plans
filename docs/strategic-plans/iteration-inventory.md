## Coverage and review limits

RSD407-ITER-2015 p.30 lists three goals and twelve objectives; pp.31–47 contain task details and pp.55–57 timeline/completion fields. Its p.54 revision record and July 2016 footer mean this copy cannot serve as an untouched June 2015 baseline. Retrospective accomplishments on pp.8–12 can be evaluated as self-reports, not assumed independently verified outcomes.

RSD407-ITER-2019 p.19 lists three goals and seven objectives; pp.20–36 provide tasks and pp.40–42 a timeline. This is an annual revision within the five-year plan, not a newly completed five-year cycle. One material target changed: reserve task 2/A/1 is 5% in the reviewed 2016 timeline (2015 source p.55) and 9% in the year-five timeline (2019 source p.40). Plan 10 must preserve the applicable target and reserve definition for each period.

The 2020–2025 school page presents high-level goals and describes monthly reporting/annual reviews plus a separate business plan; it does not expose a version-specific task register or adoption date. Its “current” label conflicts with the later district PDF's 2022–2027 title. Neither label establishes a supersession date. Treat this as unresolved publication/authority overlap, not evidence that the older plan remained governing, achieved its goals, or failed.

PDF covers were rendered and visually inspected, and the cited goal, revision and timeline sections reviewed with layout-preserving text extraction. Original PDFs stay outside the repository. Archived historical bytes have SHA-256 and original-byte replay URLs; the hash identifies the reviewed capture, with no live original available for independent comparison. New archive submission responses are recorded separately and do not prove preservation. Current 2024 PDF was re-fetched and its existing hash matched. Policy 1120 (RSD407-ITER-P1120 p.1) supports a planning process, not adoption of any particular iteration; its single-page file labels itself “Page 1 of 3,” so missing pages are not assumed.
