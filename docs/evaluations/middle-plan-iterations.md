# Middle-era plan review — 2014 bridge and 2015–2020 cycle

Plan 10 closes a bounded review of two confirmed district snapshots, not every annual revision. The [objective matrix](../../data/strategic-plans/middle-objective-matrix.json) evaluates 19 objectives; the [task register](../../data/strategic-plans/middle-task-register.json) preserves 126 numbered tasks, raw targets, deadlines, responsibilities, resource expectations and timeline marks. No overall plan-success score is justified. Research completion does not mean strategic goals were achieved.

## Authority and coverage

**MIDDLE-AUTHORITY-01 — observation.** RSD407-ITER-2015 states adoption June 9, 2015 (PDF p.1), but the reviewed 57-page copy incorporates July 11, 2016 revisions (p.54). Its twelve objectives are defined on pp.32–49, with summary p.30 and task timelines pp.55–57. RSD407-ITER-2019 is explicitly year five of 2015–2020 (cover), with seven objectives on pp.21–31 and timelines pp.40–42. The cover says May 28, 2019; the revision record p.39 says Board approval effective May 14. Neither date silently replaces the other. Original June 2015 Board motion and untouched adoption copy, intermediate 2016/2017 links, and May 2019 authority resolution were not recovered in the targeted pass.

The 2014–2015 bridge remains unresolved: RSD407-EARLY-ENTRY2014 pp.3,8 supplies transition context, and RSD407-ITER-2015 pp.8–12 reports retrospective accomplishments. Neither supplies a complete governing register or matched endpoint. The [search log](../../data/strategic-plans/middle-search-log.json) records the finite boundary, unavailable exact-URL snapshots and CDX HTTP503. Non-discovery or access failure does not prove a document never existed.

**MIDDLE-CONFLICT-01 — observation.** The year-five PDF's creation/modification metadata is February 19, 2020, not a certified year-end status date. Its retrospective section is labelled 2018–2019, yet p.34 cites certificated turnover for 2016–2017 versus 2015–2016, and the classified sentence reverses chronology while saying “down.” These numbers cannot be relabelled 2018–2019 outcomes. The 2016 summary p.30 calls objective 3B a 2016–2019 Communications Plan while its body p.46 says 2016 Communications Plan. The year-five timeline p.42 prints task code `2/5/3`; the register preserves that value and explicitly matches it to `2/B/3` using the objective text on p.26.

## Implementation and design

**MIDDLE-01 through MIDDLE-19 — evaluations.** “Reported implemented” means every numbered task has an X; “reported partial” means some have X; no X means “cannot determine.” These labels report the district's record, not independent fidelity. A blank never establishes failure or that work had not started. Broad attainment and fidelity remain unknown in all 19 rows because a matched target/baseline/coverage/endpoint chain was not established.

These are different task sets and observation windows, not a comparable performance trend. The four postponed 2016 tasks are boundary-recommendation/hearing/refinement/approval tasks 2/B/5–8 (p.56). Unmarked tasks include reports and a contract negotiation with an August 31, 2016 deadline after the July revision; they are not uniformly overdue. Year-five future May/June/August deadlines also preclude treating its blank cells as final failures.

**MIDDLE-DEADLINE-01 — observation.** Revised dates remain visible alongside original task deadlines: 2016 p.30 moves 3/B/2 September 15 to October 15; 1/C/3–5 and 3/A/2 February 15 to March 15; 3/A/4 April 15 to May 15. Year-five p.19 moves 1/A/4 January to February 2020, 1/A/1 October 2019 to March 2020, and 1/B/1 October 2019 to March 2020. These revisions do not independently establish completion or erase the earlier schedule.

Task design generally names owners, deliverables and deadlines, but progress measurements mix processes and outcomes. Examples include 100% of schools with regular Student Intervention Team meetings (2016 p.34), a 25% increase in social-media users/followers across all platforms (p.48), Tiered Fidelity Inventory results (2019 p.23), and 100% inventory/training procedures (p.27). Required denominators, cadence, baseline counts, actual fidelity scores or endpoint coverage were not acquired. Marked tasks and retrospective training/device-distribution descriptions (2019 pp.32–33) do not supply those measurements.

**MIDDLE-CROSSWALK-01 — evaluation.** Thematic continuity is not identical task lineage. The five 2016 learning objectives become two year-five objectives; fiscal/capital/safety themes continue with revised tasks and targets. Policy review 2D is absent from the current year-five list but appears in its retrospective p.33; absence is not completion or abandonment. HR changes from a 2013–2018 to 2018–2023 subplan. Communications and outreach are reorganized into year-five 3B. No code-based automatic transfer of prior completion marks or attainment is warranted.

## Matched outcome evidence

**OSPI-MIDDLE-GROWTH / MIDDLE-DERIVED-01 — observations and derived results.** The hash-verified OSPI workflow artifact contains 120 selected All Students district/state median-SGP records for 2014–2015 through 2018–2019, grades 4–8 and All Grades, ELA and math. Same-grade repeated cross-sections show nine of twelve district grade/subject comparisons declining from 2014–2015, two unchanged and one increasing. These twelve include the All Grades aggregates and are not twelve independent samples. All Grades ELA is 58→44, with endpoint WA50; math is 56→53, with endpoint WA50. Seven endpoint comparisons are below WA, five above. This is evidence relevant to the growth objective, but not a causal effect, same-pupil trajectory, average achievement measure or full 2020 endpoint. Do not average medians across grades or infer growth for every student from an aggregate.

**OSPI-MIDDLE-ASSESSMENT / MIDDLE-DERIVED-01.** Twenty-three 2018–2019 All Grades General-administration records are preserved. District All Students proficiency is 72.48358862% ELA, 62.64336428% math and 71.18881119% science, above the matched WA snapshots by 11.52237652, 12.54116597 and 18.44449449 percentage points. ELA English Language Learners, Low-Income and Students with Disabilities are respectively 61.89535333, 34.29461224 and 47.08676322 points below district All Students. That reference includes each focal group; it is not a non-focal comparison. These levels neither prove growth nor show that gaps narrowed. No matched 2015 achievement baseline or spring 2020 endpoint was acquired. The selected district English Language Learners science context has no matching record: unknown, never zero. Source `dataasof` values are retained; acquisition in 2026 does not make these 2026 outcome years.

**MIDDLE-RESERVE-01 — conditional derived result.** The [preserved finance values](../../data/strategic-plans/middle-finance-values.json) reuse audited General Fund records, with physical PDF page locators and source hashes in the finance manifest. Unassigned balance / actual expenditures yields 9.6034%, 9.3752%, 15.0258% and 16.2840% for FY2016–2017 through FY2019–2020. These exceed the numerical 5% and 9% reference levels, but 2016 p.38 varies “unassigned” and “uncommitted,” while year-five p.25 specifies 9% uncommitted. The plan denominator and maintenance throughout each year are not fully reconciled. This is not whole-objective compliance, all fiscal practices verified, or a retroactive application of the 2025 reserve rule.

**MIDDLE-BOND-01 — criterion evaluation: Not met.** Year-five 3B expressly measures a “Successful February 2020 Bond” (p.31). King County's official final results (KC-RSD407-BOND-2020 p.2, updated February 21) record 3,163 approved and 2,802 rejected, 53.03% approval. The district's Bond History independently labels its 2020 $125 million bond failed. Approval uses 5,965 votes on the measure, distinct from 5,968 ballots counted. The explicit success criterion was not met. This does not establish why it failed, whether every campaign task was performed, or that all communications work failed. Distribution and other program criteria remain unknown.

## Reproduction and closure

Reused original URLs, reviewed-byte hashes and archive results remain in [iteration sources](../../data/manifests/iteration-sources.json) and [finance sources](../../data/manifests/finance-sources.json). Two new bond sources have their own [manifest](../../data/manifests/middle-plan-sources.json): the official election PDF's submitted archive replay matches the original SHA-256 (`capture_verified`); the district HTML capture exists but hashes differ (`capture_hash_mismatch`). Previously reviewed archival PDF hashes identify those bytes and do not verify a live-original match. PDFs were reviewed locally and are not committed.

The committed OSPI values include dataset URLs, original acquisition time, artifact/run IDs, ZIP/member hashes and exact selection coverage. Artifact retention is finite; selected values support permanent recalculation. A later pull requires fresh provenance. To reproduce, obtain the original sources or recorded archive bytes and check hashes first:

```sh
python scripts/build_middle_register.py --snapshot2016 /path/to/2016-copy.pdf --yearfive2019 /path/to/year-five.pdf
python scripts/select_middle_ospi.py --artifact /path/to/validated-ospi.zip
python scripts/middle_evidence.py
```

Register reconstruction verifies both PDF hashes and expected task counts. Selection verifies the workflow ZIP hash. Diagnostics use Decimal arithmetic, exact scope/period pairing and no imputation. Verification covered deterministic JSON reproduction, unique task/objective/context keys, counts, source references and numeric comparisons; cited target/status/election and financial pages were rendered and reviewed.

The targeted authority search and one matched evidence pass are complete. Remaining authority, coverage, fidelity and full-cycle endpoint gaps are documented, not an open background investigation.
