## Recurrence findings

SYN-08 (evaluation): **Sustained responsibility** is supported for financial stewardship/reporting, safety readiness, staff learning and communication. **Evolving challenge** describes reserve recovery, allocation rules, access projects and successor HR/communications tasks (FIN-13–14, ALLOC-11, SAFE-20, HR-13, COMM-13). A new deadline or repeated task title does not independently prove prior failure.

SYN-09 (evaluation): the specific FY2017 Title I finding has qualified **Resolved or institutionalized** evidence by FY2018 (FIN-08/11), while continued reporting/control oversight remains a sustained responsibility. This classification applies to that finding, not every later federal program or every questioned-cost outcome.

SYN-10 (evaluation): **Measurement failure** is established in the public evaluation trace where target definitions conflict, execution/coverage cannot be independently tested, or published analytics comparisons cannot be reproduced as stated (FIN-02–05/13, SAFE-19–20, HR-12–13, COMM-09/12–13). It does not assert that the district has no internal measurements. Missing public records are never zero incidents, zero vacancies or zero engagement.

SYN-11 (evaluation): current low-income, disability and observable English Learner achievement disparities remain material concerns (G1-D1–D3), and the domain synthesis describes an equity challenge. This final synthesis does not infer a longitudinal widening/narrowing trend from a single current-release set, or infer recurrent implementation failure from disparities alone. **Persistent unresolved problem** as a multi-era causal/temporal classification and **Recurrent implementation failure** require stronger matched historical outcome and execution evidence than this synthesis establishes. Above-state aggregate performance does not resolve focal-group gaps.
