# 2020–2025 plan: year-one review and transition

Plan 11 completes one bounded review of the confirmed year-one document, its embedded action register, period outcomes and transition evidence. The [objective matrix](../../data/strategic-plans/transition-objective-matrix.json) covers seven objectives and fourteen goal metrics; the [raw register](../../data/strategic-plans/transition-task-register.json) preserves 34 body tasks. Broad objective attainment and fidelity remain unknown. The original 85% grade-three ELA benchmark was not met in the selected 2024–2025 state-assessment component. No overall plan-success score or causal effect is inferred.

## Authority, versions and preservation

**TRANS-AUTHORITY-01 — observation.** RSD407-TRANS-PLAN2020 is a 48-page PDF titled 2020–2025, “Year One of the Five-Year Plan 2020–2021” (p.1). Its revision record p.46 states School Board approval effective February 9, 2021; timeline p.48 is updated February 9, 2021. PDF creation/modification metadata is also February 9. A separate original Board motion and subsequent annual versions were not recovered. The nominal 2020 start must not be mistaken for a verified 2020 adoption date or a pristine pre-implementation baseline.

The document itself supplies the implementation structure: p.7 says action-plan information is included, with monthly management/Board reviews and annual February–June revision. Objectives, numbered tasks, dates, responsibilities, resources and expected ROI are on pp.23–38; timelines pp.47–48. The school HTML describes a separate business plan, but no separately titled business document was recovered. Its absence does not erase the embedded action register or justify inventing missing tasks.

**TRANS-PRESERVE-01 — observation.** Direct published links led to sign-in HTML. After the user's explicit authorization, Internet Archive captured the school link's SharePoint viewer; a file-specific download URL supplied by that viewer recovered the actual PDF. A separate PDF capture was submitted and its original-byte replay matched SHA-256 `f454eb2b71f8689c541e9a99aae4b1e7af50457f0556e7682f98272d2ff8c9ce`. The [manifest](../../data/manifests/transition-sources.json) records original published URL, capture URL, request time, reviewed hash and verification. Viewer HTML alone was never labelled verified PDF preservation. The archived 2021 district-page plan link's capture timed out; this distinct result remains recorded. PDFs were reviewed locally and are not committed. The file-specific live download URL is ephemeral; reproduction uses the stable original published link or exact archived PDF replay.

The source explicitly includes pandemic recovery/reopening in 2B (p.32). That context does not itself establish a Board-approved deadline extension or explain every missing result. Retrospective 2019–2020 accomplishments (pp.39–41) describe the previous period and cannot silently become 2020–2025 implementation evidence.

## Original goal metrics and matched tests

**TRANS-TARGET-01 through TRANS-TARGET-14 — evaluations.** The matrix preserves all fourteen metrics from pp.21,29,33 with their own wording and deadlines. A metric's inclusion in the original document does not prove it remained governing after the later plan. The following tests assess the historical specification without asserting continued authority.

**TRANS-TARGET-03 — state-assessment benchmark: Not met.** The original goal says that by 2025, 85% of grade-three students will meet/exceed ELA standards, as measured by district and state assessments (p.21). The verified OSPI 2024–2025 dataset `h5d9-vgwi` has All Students grade `03`, SBAC ELA `percent_consistent_grade` of **54.8%** for Riverview, versus **47.8%** Washington. Riverview is **30.2 percentage points below** the original benchmark despite being above the state comparison. This tests the state-assessment component. A matched district-assessment result was not acquired; neither all components nor full objective 1A are rated from this one result.

Other original metrics require more than the available proxies. The 12.9% English Learner/Special Education dual-service target needs its own population denominator and definition; ordinary EL proficiency does not test it. The 95% seniors graduating on time **with college credits or certification** target is compound; ordinary four-year cohort graduation is not the same denominator or credential criterion. ISTE incorporation in 80% of lessons needs a lesson-design denominator; all software audits and all-school PBIS Tier 1/2 at 80% fidelity need actual coverage/adherence records. The 100% annual staff-equity training target by the 2022 school year and 5% diverse-hiring increase by 2025 need their original baseline, workforce/hire definitions and coverage. Later training activities or workforce snapshots alone do not supply those chains. These targets remain “Cannot determine,” not assumed met or failed.

**TRANS-OSPI-01 / TRANS-DERIVED-01 — contextual observations and calculations.** The existing hash-verified workflow artifact supplies 60 All Grades assessment rows: five groups, district/WA, ELA/math/science, for 2021–2022 and 2024–2025, plus two matched grade-three ELA benchmark rows. Raw fractions versus percentage strings, source dates, counts, participation and suppression flags are retained. All Students ELA/math/science are respectively 71.62939297%, 56.91421255%, 63.10077519% in 2021–2022 and 63.4%, 52.4%, 58.4% in 2024–2025: decreases of approximately **8.23, 4.51 and 4.70 points**. The 2024–2025 levels exceed matched WA by 12.5, 11.7 and 15.1 points. These are repeated cross-sections with changing grade/population coverage and source precision, not student-growth percentiles, the original 2020 baseline, same-pupil change or a causal estimate. Favorable WA comparisons do not override the original 85% benchmark.

Focal groups include **Homeless**, explicitly named in original 1B, as well as EL, Low-Income and Students with Disabilities. Compare exact-period/subject/test contexts only. All Students includes the focal group; it is not a non-focal comparator. A smaller gap can arise alongside falling focal proficiency when the reference also falls. Source suppression remains visible: the EL science 2021–2022 top/bottom row is blank; the 2024–2025 row retains `7.9%` with `<8%` flag. The conservative calculation excludes both flagged contexts rather than treating either as an exact point or zero. No overall “gaps closed” verdict is justified from these snapshots.

**TRANS-FINANCE-01 — contextual derived result.** Reused audited General Fund total-balance/actual-expenditure ratios for FY2020–2021 through FY2024–2025 are **17.4406%, 14.2686%, 11.8552%, 11.0721%, 8.3887%**. The original has a **9% uncommitted** year-one task dated August 15, 2021 (p.31), and a separate 2025 aspiration to **work toward 14% uncommitted** (p.29). Total balance is not uncommitted balance; actual expenditures are an analytical denominator, not an independently resolved plan denominator. The FY2025 total-balance ratio is below the 14% numerical reference under that conditional denominator, but does not establish formal target failure or whether the district worked toward the aspiration. No 2025 revised reserve rule is back-projected, and reserve context is not proof of equitable deployment or all fiscal processes.

The original's 2025 camera-upgrade and all-staff/student Run/Hide/Fight targets need contemporaneous completion inventories and training coverage. Later current-plan safety/adoption evidence does not retrospectively prove those whole-district criteria. A proposed/presented capital bond is also different from the earlier plan's requirement for a successful bond; do not transfer Plan 10's 2020 election judgment into this different target.

Nominal 2022–2025 overlap permits no automatic causal credit or duplication of task completion.
