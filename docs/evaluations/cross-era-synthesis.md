# Cross-era strategic-plan evaluation

Bounded research is complete for the declared coverage below. This report integrates the prior-iteration reviews with the [bounded 2022–2027 report](final-synthesis.md), which retains its original scope and findings. It is an evaluation of recovered documents and evidence, not a complete census of every historical plan, district activity or student outcome. No overall attainment score is calculated.

**CROSS-SYNTHESIS-01 — evaluation.** The [machine-readable synthesis](../../data/synthesis/cross-era-evaluation.json) contains **54 version-specific objective rows**: 47 historical rows and seven objectives visible in the September 2024 current-plan snapshot. Original implementation judgments and qualifications remain intact. Seven existing domain findings are retained separately; they overlap these objectives and are not seven additional independent objectives. The specific FY2017 Title I issue is contextual evidence, not a 2022–2027 objective.

The central finding is sustained responsibility with changing scope, targets and measurement. Several narrow deliveries, a qualified audit correction and revised-policy numeric tests are supported. Two historical criteria have direct shortfall evidence. Broader objective attainment and independent full-scope fidelity remain unknown. Repeated labels, blank status cells or changed targets do not establish recurrent implementation failure.

## Recurrence classifications

**CROSS-REC-01:** **Sustained responsibility** is supported for stewardship, readiness, governance and ongoing staff learning. Dated recurring duties are expected responsibilities; repetition is not execution failure.

**CROSS-REC-02:** **Evolving challenge** describes changing equity interventions, technology, HR recruitment, communications and financial targets. The source record supports scope changes without establishing the formal disposition of every earlier task.

**CROSS-REC-03:** **Measurement failure** is supported in the public evaluation trace by documented target/denominator conflicts, mismatched body/timeline tasks, dates and analytics calculations. This does not claim the district has no internal data or measurements.

**CROSS-REC-04:** **Resolved or institutionalized**, qualified to correction of the specific FY2017 Title I issue, is supported. Permanent institutionalization or universal controls are not established.

**CROSS-REC-05:** Available repeated snapshots show an ongoing equity concern, but **Persistent unresolved problem** is not certified as an unqualified continuous all-era trend. Reconciled definitions, coverage and missing/suppressed contexts are needed for that stronger temporal claim.

**CROSS-REC-06:** **Recurrent implementation failure is not established.** It would require repeated verified execution/fidelity failure for the same intervention, with its scope and deadlines reconciled. The two narrow historical criterion shortfalls are outcome evidence, not that execution chain. This is a limit on the conclusion, not evidence that every intervention succeeded.

## Evidence quality, contrary evidence and unresolved boundaries

The synthesis uses original district PDFs/Board records, official election results, audited finance records and the validated OSPI artifact. Each is suitable for particular assertions, not all purposes. Reported X marks/training descriptions have weaker fidelity support than exact source-byte verification; hash equality verifies document identity, not the truth of every claim in the document. Missing records are never zero incidents, zero engagement, zero vacancies or proof of failure.

Contrary evidence remains visible: favorable WA achievement and later graduation coexist with declining available proficiency snapshots and large gaps; narrow numeric policy compliance coexists with declining balance levels and inconsistent reserve definitions; delivered reports and formal authorizations coexist with unknown execution, coverage and benefit. Proposed staffing/resources/ROI and forecasts remain distinct from actual deployed services or achieved returns.

Authority gaps remain material: original early registers, the 2014–2015 bridge, intermediate revisions, May 2019 dates, separate February 2021 approval motion and the precise 2022 replacement/task disposition. The 2024 report's pre-revision arithmetic (17 complete + 11 in progress versus 26) is retained; its revised task pages are a different reporting scope, not a comparable prior completion rate. There is no retrospective extension of deadlines merely because later related work appears.

## Completion and finite reopening

All known inventory candidates now have a bounded review or documented insufficient-evidence closeout. Plans 00–12 and the prior integrity follow-up are complete at their declared scopes. No background research is running or implied.

Reopen only for a concrete authoritative artifact that could change a material conclusion:

- Original adoption/register/revision or replacement record that resolves an authority/task-lineage gap.
- Defined baseline, denominator and matched outcome record for an original target; corrected source-methodology or suppression record for a specific comparison.
- Dated execution, coverage/fidelity, acceptance or corrective-action record for a named task, rather than a related later activity.
- Corrected reserve/analytics definitions or calculations, reconciled deployed staffing/service access, or final disposition of a specific audit issue.

A new title, forecast or broadly available dataset is not itself a reason to restart collection. Research completion means completion of this evidence-bounded evaluation, not district-wide strategic-goal attainment.
