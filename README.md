# RSD407 Strategic Plan Evaluation

This repository is an external, evidence-based review of Riverview School District No. 407's strategic plans: what the district intended to achieve, what work is documented, what outcomes can be tested, and what changed across plan versions. It brings district documents, official outcome data and reproducible calculations together so readers can examine the evidence behind each judgment.

## What did the review find?

**Mixed, partial progress. Broad strategic-goal attainment is not demonstrated by the reviewed evidence.** This is not a finding that every goal failed or that additional district evidence does not exist.

## A core finding: many tasks could not be independently measured

**Many reviewed tasks and objectives lacked sufficient evidence to verify completion, implementation coverage or effectiveness.** The gaps were not limited to missing outcome scores: they included undefined success criteria, absent baselines or denominators, unavailable completion/fidelity records, incompatible reporting periods and conflicting task or measure definitions. These problems limit the public record's ability to demonstrate whether commitments were fulfilled ([CROSS-REC-03](docs/evaluations/cross-era-synthesis.md#recurrence-classifications)).

| Measurement problem | Concrete example and consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Completion labels without enough supporting evidence | Safety procedures and training descriptions did not supply dated coverage, completed drill/after-action results or comparable safety outcomes. A reported Completed label could not establish operational readiness or full implementation. [SAFE-14 / SAFE-19](docs/evaluations/goal-2-safety-operations.md) |
| Activities without a defined effectiveness test | HR and professional-learning records documented events and opportunities, but did not establish current workforce match, retention improvements, universal training coverage or application. Activity delivery and attainment of the intended benefit remained separate. [HR-11 / HR-12](docs/evaluations/goal-3-human-resources.md) |
| Published metrics that could not be reproduced as stated | Communications growth claims used unclear intervals, visitor categories or denominators; screenshot arithmetic did not validate the reported growth interpretation. Report delivery did not demonstrate increased reach or understanding. [COMM-05 / COMM-09–10](docs/evaluations/goal-3-communications.md) |
| Conflicting targets and version records | Reserve specifications changed, and some historical body/timeline task descriptions conflicted. Readers could not consistently connect the governing commitment to its result. [FIN-02–05](docs/evaluations/goal-2-finance-evidence-synthesis.md) / [TRANS-CONFLICT-01](docs/evaluations/plan-2020-2025.md) |

**This is a substantive accountability issue, not just a limitation of the review.** Where a reported accomplishment cannot be connected to a defined measure and supporting result, families and the Board cannot independently verify its extent or effectiveness from that record. The evidence supports a finding of measurement failure in the public evaluation trace. It does not prove the district has no internal data, that every task is unmeasurable or that undocumented work failed.

## Other substantive findings

| Finding | Evidence and qualification |
| --- | --- |
| Positive academic outcomes | Current achievement compares favorably with Washington, and four-year graduation improved relative to the reviewed 2018–2019 baseline. These results do not establish attainment of every strategic target. [G1-A / G1-C](docs/evaluations/goal-1-evidence-synthesis.md) |
| Two specific historical criteria were missed | The February 2020 bond failed. Grade-three ELA proficiency in 2024–2025 was 54.8%, below the original 85% benchmark's state-assessment component; later replacement authority remains unresolved. These are narrower findings, not whole-plan failure ratings. [MIDDLE-BOND-01 / TRANS-TARGET-03](docs/evaluations/cross-era-synthesis.md#narrow-findings-that-should-not-be-lost) |
| Achievement disparities remain | Available evidence shows substantial focal-group disparities. Some ELA gaps narrowed while a disability gap widened; a narrower gap can coexist with declining proficiency. [G1-D](docs/evaluations/goal-1-evidence-synthesis.md) / [CROSS-ELA-1–5](docs/evaluations/cross-era-synthesis.md#outcome-levels-gaps-and-washington-are-different-tests) |
| Financial success has a defined scope | FY2025 revised-policy reserve thresholds were met, while balances declined for four consecutive years after FY2021. Those policy tests do not establish attainment of differently defined strategic reserve targets. [FIN-04 / FIN-10 / FIN-13](docs/evaluations/goal-2-finance-evidence-synthesis.md) |
| Work is documented; broad benefits often remain unverified | Authorizations, reports and partial implementation are supported in allocation, safety, workforce and communications. Full coverage, effectiveness and broad outcome attainment remain uncertain. [SYN-04–07](docs/evaluations/final-synthesis.md#master-evaluation-matrix) |

## Why does this matter?

The practical question for families, taxpayers and the Board is whether reported activity demonstrates the promised result. Without defined measures and comparable supporting evidence, it is difficult to distinguish successful delivery from partial work, assess who benefited, or determine what needs correction. A completed task, an above-state comparison and attainment of an original goal are different tests ([SYN-10](docs/evaluations/final-synthesis.md#recurrence-findings)).

The review provides a basis for asking:

- What was the governing target, baseline and deadline?
- What evidence verifies completion, implementation coverage and the resulting outcome?
- If a target or task changed, how was the original commitment resolved?

Documented conflicts and missing measurement chains limit public verification of success ([CROSS-REC-03](docs/evaluations/cross-era-synthesis.md#recurrence-classifications)). The evidence supports scrutiny of particular results and reporting gaps; it does not support a blanket claim of repeated implementation failure or a defensible overall percentage of goals met.

## Evaluation scope

The synthesis covers **54 version-specific objective rows**, with partial historical registers and unrecovered versions explicitly identified. All 54 broad objective-attainment judgments remain **Cannot determine**; narrower successes and misses are retained separately. They cannot be turned into a reliable overall success rate ([CROSS-SYNTHESIS-01](docs/evaluations/cross-era-synthesis.md#exactly-what-was-reviewed)).

The current district plan cycle continues through 2027. Concrete material evidence can change conclusions under the final report's [reopening conditions](docs/evaluations/cross-era-synthesis.md#completion-and-finite-reopening).
